Use

  • Clear space around the mark: at least the capsule height on every side.
  • Bare mark: the standard cut from 48 px wide, the small cut from 20 to 47 px. Below 20 px, or on unknown backgrounds, use the favicon tile.
  • Keep the capsule's left half in outline and right half filled. Do not recolour the capsule to mimic a real product.
  • No red cross, check mark, shield or certification device. Open Med Tray is research software and makes no clinical claim.
  • The product is Open Med Tray, with no qualifier. Lite is the only edition name. Blender is named only where setup or compatibility requires it, never in a lockup, and its logo is never used.
The wordmark is set in IBM Plex Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1, see brand/licenses/IBM-Plex-fonts.LICENSE.txt). SVG lockups use live text and need IBM Plex Sans installed; the PNG lockups are portable. The mark and icons are pure vector paths. This page embeds IBM Plex under the same licence.
